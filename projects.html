NBA Workload & Performance Analysis

Category: Data Science and Statistical Analysis

Methods: Exploratory Data Analysis, Statistical Testing, Regression

This project analyzed 26,651 NBA player-game logs to investigate how heavy playing minutes and short rest periods relate to shooting efficiency and player availability.

Key Contributions

  • Analyzed 26,651 NBA player-game records.
  • Applied statistical testing and regression analysis.
  • Investigated relationships between workload and performance.
  • Found that heavy-workload players did not show lower availability, suggesting possible selection bias.
